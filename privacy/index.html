Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

The short version

  • Dayquarto does not collect your personal data. We never see your entries, activities, moods, gifts, photos or books.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your journal is stored locally on your device.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Backups and PDF exports are created only when you ask, and you choose where they go.

This policy explains how the Dayquarto app for iPhone and iPad ("Dayquarto", "the app") and this website handle information. "We" and "us" refer to the developer of Dayquarto.

Information we collect

None. Dayquarto has no account system, and we don't run servers that receive your data. Everything you write in the app stays on your device unless you choose to save a backup or export a book.

Data stored on your device

To work, Dayquarto saves what you create in the app's private storage on your device. This includes:

  • Journal entries and their times, including entries you mark private
  • Moods, day marks, highlights and highlight categories
  • Activities, what you recorded for them, and their notes
  • Logged times, such as when you woke up or went to bed
  • To-dos, week and month summaries, and your yearly notes
  • Gifts, including names, occasions, stories and photos you add
  • Your books and their settings
  • App settings such as the app lock, the daily reminder time and the day your week starts

We cannot access this data. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data from your device. To keep a copy, save a backup first.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include Dayquarto's data in those device backups, as it does for most apps. Apple's terms and your backup settings govern those backups.

Permissions the app asks for

Dayquarto asks for a permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it. You can change these permissions any time in the iOS Settings app.

PermissionWhat it's used for
Face ID To unlock Dayquarto when you turn on the app lock. iOS handles authentication. Dayquarto never receives your Face ID data or your device passcode; it only learns whether unlocking succeeded.
Notifications To show the daily reminder, if you turn it on. The reminder is scheduled on your device, and no information is sent anywhere.
Photos When you add a photo to a gift, Dayquarto uses the system photo picker, so it sees only the photos you choose. Each chosen photo is copied into the app on your device. Dayquarto doesn't read or upload the rest of your library.

If you decline a permission, the rest of the app keeps working. Only the feature that needs it is unavailable.

Private entries and the app lock

Entries you mark private stay out of your books and PDF exports unless you choose to include them. The app lock also hides your journal in the app switcher. Private entries are still included in backups, so a backup holds your whole journal.

Backups and exports

Dayquarto can save a backup of your journal as a .dayquarto file, and export your books as PDFs. These files are created only when you ask. You choose where they go, for example the Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or Messages.

Once a file leaves the app, the policies of the place you sent it to apply. Backup files contain your whole journal, including private entries, so keep them somewhere you trust. You can restore a backup in Dayquarto to bring your journal back, for example on a new phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data. We don't have it.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs from third parties.

Information Apple may share with developers

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give developers crash reports and app usage statistics. Apple says these don't identify you. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Dayquarto, and you can turn it off at any time.

Children

Dayquarto doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

dayquarto.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and it doesn't load fonts or scripts from other services. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to run its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Dayquarto's data practices change, for example if a sync feature is added, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@dayquarto.com. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

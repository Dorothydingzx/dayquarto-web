Daily journal & yearly books for iPhone

Write a line. Mark your day. Keep a book of your year.

Dayquarto is a journal for ordinary days. Jot down what happened, tap off what you did, and note how you felt. Over the year, those small entries become a book you can read in the app or export as a PDF.

Small entries, gathered into a book

One line counts. There are no streaks, no scores and nothing telling you that you missed a day. Quiet days belong in the book too.

Write a little, as often as you like

  • Add short entries through the day. Each one is timestamped and kept on that day's page.
  • Pick a mood, and mark birthdays, anniversaries, holidays and travel.
  • Star the entries that matter most and sort highlights into your own categories.
  • Keep a list of gifts you gave and received, with photos and the story behind them.

Track what you do, your way

  • Create your own activities and record each one as a checkmark, a duration or a count.
  • Add a short note, like “Upper body, 30 minutes”, and log times such as when you woke up.
  • See each week, a monthly calendar with to-dos and heatmaps, and a summary of your whole year.

Your year, as a book

  • Every year becomes a book: a cover, the year in brief, your best moments, twelve monthly chapters and a closing note.
  • The current year is a growing draft, so you don't have to wait until December.
  • Choose a design and a title, then read it in the app or export a PDF to save or print.

Take a look inside

A page for today, a view of every week, month and year, and a shelf of books that keeps growing.

Your journal stays yours

Dayquarto is built so that your journal lives on your iPhone, not on someone else's server. No ads, no analytics and no tracking.

No account

No sign-up, no login, no email address. Open the app and write.

Stored on your device

Entries, activities, moods, gifts and books are saved locally on your device.

Locked and private

Lock Dayquarto with Face ID or your passcode, and mark entries private to keep them out of your book.

You hold the backup

Save a backup to Files or iCloud Drive whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Dayquarto has no sign-up or login. Install it and start writing.

Where is my journal stored?

On your device. Your entries, activities, moods, gifts and books are saved locally and never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my journal or move it to a new iPhone?

Open Settings and tap Save a backup under Backup. This makes a .dayquarto file that holds your whole journal, including private entries. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. On the other device, install Dayquarto, open Settings and tap Restore from a backup. Restoring replaces everything in the app with exactly what the backup holds; your settings aren't changed. Because your journal isn't stored on our servers, a backup file is how you keep a copy.

What happens to my journal if I delete the app?

iOS deletes Dayquarto's data from your device when you delete the app. Save a backup first if you want to keep your journal. Backups and PDFs you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

How do I export my book as a PDF?

Open Gallery and tap a year's book. In the book reader, tap the share button to export a PDF, then save or print it. The current year's book is a draft that grows as you write, so you can export it at any time.

What does marking an entry private do?

Private entries stay out of your books and PDF exports unless you choose to include them. Tap the lock while writing, or press and hold an entry, to make it private. Private entries are still included in backups.

How does the app lock work?

Turn on Lock with Face ID in Settings under Privacy. Dayquarto then asks for Face ID, or your device passcode, when you open it, and hides your journal in the app switcher. The lock needs a device passcode; if you haven't set one, turn it on in the iOS Settings app first.

Why don't I get the daily reminder?

Turn on Daily reminder in Settings and pick a time. If it still doesn't appear, allow notifications for Dayquarto in the iOS Settings app under Notifications.

Does Dayquarto work on iPad, and offline?

Yes to both. Dayquarto runs on iPhone and iPad, and because your journal is stored on your device, you can write, read your books and export PDFs without an internet connection.
